# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

EDMissions is designed for one higher-education enrollment professional working through a daily mix of news monitoring, research capture, campaign drafting, task management, and focused individual work.

## Product Purpose

EDMissions is a daily command center for focused work, current enrollment signals, research, and campaign drafting. It succeeds when it makes staying informed and producing useful work engaging enough to become a daily habit.

## Positioning

A higher-ed enrollment command center that makes staying informed and producing work energizing enough to become a daily habit.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, focused, and inventive, with a slightly rebellious edge. The product should feel confident and alive without losing the clarity required for serious enrollment work.

## Anti-references

EDMissions must not resemble generic corporate administration software, a templated AI or SaaS dashboard, a chaotic rave flyer that sacrifices usability, or a cute and gamified productivity app.

## Design Principles

- Make daily enrollment work worth opening by pairing practical tools with genuine energy.
- Keep the work primary: expression should reinforce focus, comprehension, and momentum.
- Connect news, research, notes, campaigns, and tasks into one coherent individual workflow.
- Prefer focused personal utility over team features, enterprise ceremony, or speculative complexity.
- Use a rebellious edge as personality, never as an excuse for visual disorder.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target is required while EDMissions remains a personal tool. Revisit accessibility requirements if the product expands to additional users.
